Data Deletion

How to request deletion of your Storyteller data

Last updated: 1st August 2026

This page explains how to delete data associated with your Storyteller account and app usage, in line with Google Play's Data Safety requirements. It complements our Privacy Policy.

Current version: Storyteller only offers anonymous sign-in (no email, password, or name is collected). Your account is identified only by a random ID, so most users have nothing to "delete" beyond uninstalling the app - but if you used "Sync now", some learning data may exist on our servers, and you can request its removal below.

1. Data you can remove yourself, right now

  • Uninstall the app - removes all local, on-device data immediately: downloaded stories/audio/dictionary files, reading settings, local vocabulary/progress not yet synced, and any daily-reminder schedule.
  • Never tap "Sync now" - if you'd rather keep everything local-only, simply don't use the sync feature; no learning data is ever sent to our servers.

2. Requesting deletion of synced data

If you have used "Sync now" at least once, the following data may be stored under your anonymous account in our database:

  • Saved vocabulary words
  • Reading progress per story
  • Playlists (including Favorites)
  • Quiz history
  • Reading/app settings (font size, theme, UI language)
  • Learning stats (streaks, daily activity, badges earned)
  • Game high scores (if applicable)
To request deletion:
  1. Email origvu@gmail.com with the subject line Data deletion request.
  2. Include the approximate date you used the app and, if possible, the device/platform (Android) - this helps us locate the right anonymous account, since no email or name is tied to it.
  3. We will delete the associated Firestore data within 30 days of receiving your request and confirm by email once complete.

3. What gets deleted vs. what may remain

  • Deleted: all data listed in Section 2, tied to the account/device you identify.
  • May remain briefly: anonymized crash reports and aggregate analytics events, which are not linked to your identity and are retained per Firebase's standard diagnostic retention windows.

4. Future: in-app account deletion

If a future update adds email/password sign-in, Storyteller will provide a "Delete account" option directly in the app (Account screen), which immediately removes your Firebase Authentication account and all associated Firestore data without needing to email us. This page will be updated when that ships.
