Privacy Policy

Storyteller - a reading & listening app for learning English

Last updated: 1st August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Storyteller ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Storyteller is built for reading and listening to stories, with an on-device dictionary and English-learning tools. Most content and learning features run entirely on your device.

1. Information We Collect

CategoryWhat it isWhen it's collected
Anonymous account identifier A random ID (Firebase Authentication, anonymous sign-in). No email, password, name, or other personal identifier is collected in the current version of the app. When you open Account & choose to continue without an account, or use "Sync now".
Synced learning data If you tap "Sync now": your saved vocabulary words, reading progress, playlists (including Favorites), quiz history, reading/app settings (font size, theme, UI language), learning stats (streaks, daily activity), and badges earned. Only when you manually trigger "Sync now" from the Account or My Progress screen. Sync is never automatic.
Crash & diagnostic data Automatically collected crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) - stack traces, device model, OS version, app version. No message you typed or word you looked up is included. Automatically, if the app crashes or hits an error, on mobile builds only.
Analytics data Aggregate usage data (Firebase Analytics) - screens viewed, feature usage, session length, device/locale info, and an app-instance identifier. Automatically, during normal use.
We do not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, or photos. Storyteller does not sell your data.

Microphone (pronunciation practice)

If you use the flashcard "record & compare" pronunciation feature, the app requests microphone access to record your voice for local playback only. This recording is never uploaded or transmitted anywhere - it stays on your device and is discarded when you're done practicing.

Advertising

The current version of the app does not show advertisements. If ads are enabled in a future update, this policy will be updated first, and any ad network used (AppLovin MAX mediation) will be disclosed here along with its own data practices.

2. How We Use Information

  • To let you access your saved words, reading progress, and playlists across your devices when you use "Sync now".
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs.
  • To understand which features are used, so we can improve the app.

3. Where Data Is Stored

Synced data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase), scoped so that each account can only read and write its own data - enforced by server-side security rules, not just app-side checks. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Data not explicitly synced (e.g. downloaded audio files, download preferences, local notification settings) stays only on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following service providers, each acting as a data processor on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics, Analytics, Remote Config) - Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare - hosts static story content (text, images, audio) delivered over a public CDN; no personal data is sent to this service.

5. Data Retention

Synced data is retained until you request deletion (see below) or until your anonymous account is inactive for an extended period, at which point it may be periodically purged. Crash and analytics data is retained per Firebase's standard retention windows.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • Don't sync: if you never tap "Sync now", no learning data leaves your device.
  • Delete your data: see our Data Deletion instructions for how to request removal of any data associated with your account.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the app removes all local, on-device data (downloads, settings, local vocabulary/progress not yet synced).

7. Children's Privacy

Storyteller is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details below and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves (for example, if we add email sign-in, advertising, or purchases). We will update the "Last updated" date above when changes are made. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
